5-Ingredient Easy Chocolate Chip Cookies

Home -> desserts -> 5 minutes cookie

These easy chocolate chip cookies are made with just 5 ingredients in under 20 minutes for a delicious treat. This recipe makes 2 dozen soft and cake-like cookies. I highly recommend eating these cookies warm!

Submitted by Shanyu Zeng Updated on Sep 19, 2026

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 10 minutes

Total Time: 20 minutes

Servings: 24 side servings

Jump to Nutrition facts

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 1 large eggs
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • cup semisweet chocolate chips

Steps:

  • Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  • Beat brown sugar and butter together in a large bowl with an electric mixer until light and fluffier in texture. Beat in eggs, one at a time. Gradually stir in flour until well combined; fold in chocolate chips.
  • Drop dough by rounded tablespoons 2 inches apart onto ungreased baking sheets. Bake in the preheated oven until the edges are golden brown, 10 to 15 minutes. Cool cookies on baking sheets for a few minutes before transferring to wire racks. (remember to leave some chocolate chips to leave at the top of the cookies)
