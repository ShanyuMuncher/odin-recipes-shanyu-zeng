Easy One-Pot Jambalaya

Home -> Main Course -> Ease One-Pot Jambalaya

This easy jambalaya is made in one pot with chicken, sausage, rice, vegetables, and bold Cajun seasoning. It’s a warm, filling dinner that comes together in under an hour.

Submitted by Shanyu Zeng Updated on Sep 20, 2026

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Total Time: 50 minutes

Servings: 6 side servings

Jump to Nutrition facts

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 12 ounces smoked sausage, sliced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 1 can (14½ ounces) diced tomatoes
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons Cajun seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 2 green onions, sliced, for garnish

Steps:

  • Gather the ingredients. Heat the olive oil in a large, deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
  • Add the chicken and sausage. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is lightly browned. Transfer the meat to a plate.
  • Add the onion, bell pepper, and celery to the pot. Cook for about 5 minutes, until softened. Stir in the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  • Stir in the rice, diced tomatoes, chicken broth, Cajun seasoning, thyme, and bay leaf. Return the chicken and sausage to the pot and bring the mixture to a boil.
  • Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, until the rice is tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed. Avoid lifting the lid or stirring too often while the rice cooks.
  • Remove the bay leaf. Season with salt and black pepper, then let the jambalaya rest for 5 minutes. Fluff gently and garnish with sliced green onions. Serve warm
