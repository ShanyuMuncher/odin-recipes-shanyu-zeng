Easy Greek Salad

Home -> Appitizer -> Greek Salad

This fresh Greek salad is made with crisp vegetables, Kalamata olives, feta cheese, and a simple homemade dressing. It comes together in just 15 minutes and makes a refreshing side dish or light lunch.

Submitted by Shanyu Zeng · Updated on September 19, 2026

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 0 minutes

Total Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4 side servings

Jump to Nutrition facts

Ingredients

  • 4 medium tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 cucumber, sliced into half-moons
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup pitted Kalamata olives
  • 150 g feta cheese, cut into cubes or served as a slab

For the dressing

  • 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Wash the tomatoes, cucumber, and bell pepper. Cut the vegetables and thinly slice the red onion.
  2. Place the tomatoes, cucumber, onion, bell pepper, and olives in a large bowl.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, oregano, and black pepper.
  4. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss gently. Add the feta on top.
  5. Taste and add a little salt if needed, keeping in mind that the feta and olives are already salty. Serve fresh.

Serving Tips

Serve with crusty bread to soak up the dressing. If preparing ahead, keep the dressing and feta separate and add them just before serving.
